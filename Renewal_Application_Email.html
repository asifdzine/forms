Dear Applicant,

This is to inform you that your certificate for Application Number [Application Number] is due for renewal on [Renewal Date].

We kindly request you to apply for the renewal before the due date to avoid any interruption or delay.

For your convenience, if most of your information remains the same, you may follow the steps below to submit a renewal application quickly:

  1. Log in to your account
  2. Go to the Renewal tab from the left menu
  3. Locate the application you wish to renew
  4. Click on the three dots (Action button)
  5. Select “Create Application from Old Application”

A new application form will open with your previous data pre-filled. Please review and modify the information as required, upload the necessary attachments, and submit the application.

If you require any assistance, please feel free to contact us.
